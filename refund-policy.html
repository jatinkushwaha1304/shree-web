Refund & Return Policy

Hassle-free returns for a better shopping experience

At ShreeJee Ayucare, we aim to provide you with a smooth and worry-free shopping experience. While we take every step to ensure the quality of our products, we understand that sometimes returns or exchanges may be necessary. Our policy is designed to be simple and fair for our customers.

1. For Products Shipped by Us

If you wish to return a product, you may do so within 7 days of receiving your order. To be eligible for a return, the product must be unused, in its original condition, with all tags intact, and in its original box/packaging. Please note that temperature-sensitive items are not eligible for returns.

2. For Products Shipped Directly by Third-Party Sellers

Products shipped directly by third-party sellers are not eligible for return through us. However, we will assist you in coordinating with the seller to find a suitable solution. Just email us at shreejeeayucare@gmail.com and we will take care of the rest. Products shipped by sellers will be clearly marked on the product detail page.

3. For Products Not Yet Shipped

You can cancel your order within 24 hours of purchase by writing to us at shreejeeayucare@gmail.com. If the order has not been packed or shipped, we will cancel it and issue a full refund. For orders that are already shipped, our standard return policy will apply.

4. For Products Damaged in Transit

We pack every order with care, but in rare cases, items may be damaged during transit. If you receive a damaged package, please do not accept it and request the courier to return it to us. Email us at shreejeeayucare@gmail.com and we will send you a replacement right away.
