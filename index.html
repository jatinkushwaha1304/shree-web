Diacare Powder is a natural herbal supplement by ShreeJee, formulated to support healthy blood sugar levels by stimulating insulin production naturally.
How do I use it?
Mix 1-2 teaspoons of Diacare Powder with water or your favorite beverage, and consume once daily, preferably in the morning.
Is it safe for everyone?
It is generally safe for most adults, but consult a healthcare professional if you are pregnant, nursing, or on medication.
How long will it take to see results?
Results may vary, but many users notice improvements in blood sugar levels within 2-4 weeks of regular use.
Can it replace my diabetes medication?
No, it is a supplement to support health, not a substitute for prescribed medication. Consult your doctor before making changes.
